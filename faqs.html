Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

All your Questions answered in one place
What services does FAO Dental Clinic offer?
FAO Dental Clinic provides a full range of dental services including routine cleanings, fillings, root canals, crowns, bridges, teeth whitening, orthodontics (braces and aligners), and dental implants. We also offer preventive care and pediatric dentistry for children.
How often should I visit the dentist?
It’s recommended to visit the dentist every six months for a routine check-up and cleaning. However, patients with gum disease, braces, or other ongoing treatments may need more frequent visits.
What should I do in case of a dental emergency?
If you experience a dental emergency such as a knocked-out tooth, severe toothache, or broken dental work, contact FAO Dental Clinic immediately. We offer emergency appointments and will guide you on what to do until you arrive.
Does FAO Dental Clinic accept insurance or offer payment plans?
Yes, FAO Dental Clinic accepts most major dental insurance plans. For patients without insurance, We offer flexible payment options and financing plans to make dental care accessible and affordable.
Is teeth whitening safe and effective?
Yes, professional teeth whitening at FAO Dental Clinic is safe, effective, and supervised by dental professionals. It provides faster and more noticeable results compared to over-the-counter products, with minimal sensitivity.
Do you treat children at FAO Dental Clinic?
Yes, FAO Dental Clinic offers comprehensive pediatric dental care. Our friendly team is experienced in working with children of all ages, making visits fun and stress-free while focusing on early prevention, education, and gentle treatment.
How do I book an appointment?
Booking an appointment is easy! You can call the clinic directly or click on the link to schedule online, or walk in to check availability. For faster service, it’s best to book ahead—especially for weekend or evening slots.
