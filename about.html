About the FAO Clinic

Welcome to FAO Dental Clinic, where your smile is our passion and your oral health is our priority. Located in a serene and accessible part of the city, FAO Dental Clinic is a modern, patient-centered dental facility committed to delivering high-quality, affordable dental care in a comfortable and friendly environment.

Our team is made up of highly skilled and experienced dental professionals-dentists, hygienists, and support staff who are dedicated to excellence. Whether you're visiting us for a routine cleaning, cosmetic treatment, or a complex dental procedure, you can trust that you're in good hands.

We offer a wide range of services including:

  • General and Preventive Dentistry
  • Cosmetic Dentistry (Whitening, Veneers)
  • Orthodontics (Braces and Aligners)
  • Pediatric Dentistry
  • Tooth Replacement (Crowns, Bridges, Implants)
  • Emergency Dental Care

We understand that visiting the dentist can be stressful for some, which is why we've designed our clinic to be a calm and relaxing space. From the moment you walk through our doors, you'll be greeted with a warm smile and treated with the utmost respect and care.

Clinic Director

Dr Wole Olusanya

Dr. Wole Olusanya is the visionary leader behind FAO Dental Clinic, bringing over 25 years of experience in clinical dentistry, surgical care, and patient advocacy. Known for their calm demeanor and meticulous approach, Dr. Wole Olusanya has earned the trust of thousands of patients across Nigeria and beyond. Their leadership has transformed FAO Dental Clinic into a center of excellence for modern dental care.

Expertise and Specializations:

Dr. Wole Olusanya specializes in:
  • Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery
  • Restorative and Cosmetic Dentistry
  • Dental Implantology
  • Preventive and Pediatric Dentistry
Their medical focus is on delivering minimally invasive, evidence-based treatments that restore both function and aesthetics. Whether managing complex surgical cases or routine dental checkups, Dr. Wole Olusanya ensures every patient receives personalized, high-quality care.

Medical Philosophy:

Dr. Wole Olusanya believes that dentistry is not just about teeth—it’s about people. Their philosophy centers on:
  • Patient-Centric Care: Listening first, treating second. Every treatment plan is tailored to the individual’s needs, lifestyle, and long-term health goals.
  • Comfort and Trust: Creating a safe, welcoming environment where patients feel heard and respected.
  • Education and Empowerment: Helping patients understand their oral health so they can make informed decisions.
This compassionate approach has made FAO Dental Clinic a preferred destination for families, professionals, and international visitors seeking world-class dental care.
